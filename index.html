FAQ

Do I need Excel or coding skills?

No. Every template comes with step-by-step instructions. You only fill in the highlighted cells.

Does it work in Google Sheets or Excel?

Templates are built for Google Sheets. Where possible, an Excel version is included too — check each product page.

Can I customize the template?

Yes. After copying, it's your file: rename columns, add categories, change colors.

Where is my data stored?

In your own Google account. TableFlow never sees or stores your data.
